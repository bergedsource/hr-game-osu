You're the HR Manager

Eight corrective-action cases are on your desk: missed shifts, skipped trainings, a broken machine, a friend who keeps showing up late. Every ruling moves three meters. Decide each one, then see where you land as a negotiator.

ComplianceDid the company get what the policy is for: the coverage, the training, the work.
RelationshipsHow the employee, and the coworkers watching, feel about how you treated them.
PrecedentWhether your rulings are consistent enough that the next person can predict them.

Keyboard: press 1 to 4 to pick a ruling, Enter for the next case, U to undo. Cases are fictional. Each is a workplace version of a settlement type from a student organization's discipline records, with no names or identifying details. The comparison figures are that organization's Winter 2026 records. Made by Dillon Berge for MGMT 455: Influence & Negotiation.
